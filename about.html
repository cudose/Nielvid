About us

Compliant people, practical training, reliable supply

Nielvid Services Limited helps regulated and project-driven organizations operate with confidence by providing compliant manpower, industry-ready training, and reliable procurement services.

Company profile

Who we are

Nielvid was founded to deliver dependable manpower, standards-aligned training, and transparent procurement to operators who cannot afford to compromise on compliance.

We bring six integrated service lines under one roof: procurement and supply chain, technical and engineering support, equipment leasing and rental, manpower and mobilisation, training and competency, and marine logistics and offshore support, with proper documentation at every step.

Our team brings experience from oil & gas, marine, and industrial operations, and we stay close to every engagement from first briefing to final delivery.
